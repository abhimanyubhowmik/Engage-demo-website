Thirteen doctoral projects, six academic groups and more than ten companies: ENGAGE is a big network, and its research is easiest to understand through the people doing it. That is why every doctoral candidate now has a dedicated profile page.

Each profile introduces the candidate’s scientific background and research project, shows who supervises the work at the academic host and the industrial partner, and places the project within the network’s four research objectives — perception and 3D mapping, learning-based control, safe autonomy, and simulation intelligence.

## Explore by industry

The projects span the industries where mobile working machines matter most. Browse the [doctoral candidates](/doctoral-candidates/) and filter by research theme, host country or industry — from excavators on construction sites and haul roads in mining, to forest machines and autonomous tractors.

Profiles will grow over the coming years with publications, datasets, secondment stories and news from each project.

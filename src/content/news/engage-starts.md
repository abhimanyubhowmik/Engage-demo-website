Mobile working machines are ubiquitous in mining, construction, harbours, forestry, and farming, and vital for the economic and industrial advancement of our society — and as such key to its sustainability and productivity.

Ambitious goals for sustainability, productivity, and safety, coupled with a shortage of human resources, have prompted the European mobile working machines ecosystem to come together in this network of Industrial Doctorates. Running from October 2025 to September 2029, ENGAGE will train a new generation of engineers to build next-generation mobile working machines for green and autonomous operations.

## Industry and academia, side by side

Key European machine manufacturers and technology suppliers have joined forces with academic groups highly specialised in AI, robotics, and mechanics from Finland, Germany, Sweden, and Austria. Each doctoral candidate is supervised jointly by an academic host and an industrial partner.

## Why now

Europe’s mobile working machine industry needs to invest heavily in digitalisation and green, innovative technologies. There is a rapidly growing demand for experts who have a holistic overview of trends in sustainability and safety, understand the challenges and opportunities that robotics and AI bring, and are trained in disciplines such as robotics and AI, machinery, domain knowledge, and the big trends of digitalisation, sustainability and responsible AI. ENGAGE addresses these needs through research and training.

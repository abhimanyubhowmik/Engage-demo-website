background: |
  My academic journey began with a B.E. in Control Engineering at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST), Vietnam. During my undergraduate studies, I gained research experience as a remote intern at the Dynamic Robotics and Control Lab (DRCL), University of Southern California (USC), USA.

  I later earned an M.Sc. in Information Communication Convergence Technology from Soongsil University, South Korea, where I specialised in 3D object detection for autonomous driving. Before starting my Ph.D. at the Technical University of Munich (TUM) under the MSCA ENGAGE Doctoral Network, I worked as an AI Researcher at DeltaX in Seoul. My research focuses on 3D perception, object detection, object tracking, scene understanding, and multimodal perception for robotics and autonomous systems.
project: |
  This project aims to develop AI-driven 3D perception methods for robust real-time understanding of dynamic environments. The research focuses on multimodal sensor fusion using LiDAR, cameras, and radar, combined with foundation models for object detection, tracking, and semantic scene understanding.

  It also investigates self-learning, language-guided reasoning, and efficient deployment on resource-constrained edge devices for autonomous mobile machines.

  My academic journey began at the University of Bologna, where I earned a bachelor’s degree in Automation Engineering. I then moved to Genoa to pursue a master’s degree in Robotics Engineering at the University of Genoa, where I built a strong academic foundation in robot programming, autonomous navigation, SLAM and perception.

  Towards the end of my studies, I participated in the Erasmus+ Traineeship Programme, conducting my master’s thesis research at the ARIES Laboratory of Heidelberg University.
project: |
  My research focuses on developing a pipeline for automatically updating as-built models for infrastructure worksites. The proposed approach integrates data collected from a sensor box mounted on a heavy-duty machine to generate a reliable 3D semantic map of the environment, which is then used to automatically produce an up-to-date as-built model with minimal human intervention.

  The main challenges include robust perception in unstructured environments, multimodal sensor data fusion, and reliable semantic scene understanding.

  I received my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Koç University in Istanbul, Türkiye in 2023. I then completed my M.Sc. in the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master’s Program in Intelligent Field Robotic Systems at the University of Girona (Spain), Eötvös Loránd University (Budapest, Hungary), and the University of Zagreb (Croatia).

  I worked on urban object detection using sensor fusion for autonomous navigation in my master’s thesis.
project: |
  My research focuses on developing methods to evaluate terrain accessibility and drivability using digital environment maps derived from available sensor data, combining semantic and geometric information to characterise the environment.

  A central aim is to quantify surface deformation severity relative to a given machine’s off-road capability. Building on this, the project addresses optimal planning of corrective measures without disrupting the primary work task.

background: |
  My academic journey began at the University of Naples Federico II, where I earned a Bachelor’s degree in Automation Engineering.

  I then moved to Politecnico di Milano to pursue a Master’s degree in Automation and Control Engineering, where I built a strong academic foundation in advanced control theory, numerical optimisation, and physics-informed machine learning.
project: |
  This research aims to develop learning-based control methods for mobile working machines that generalise across machine types, tasks, and environments, addressing the sim-to-real gap through improved simulation environments and transfer techniques such as domain randomisation.

  It investigates expressive, diffusion-based policies trained via reinforcement learning to capture the multimodal, contact-rich dynamics of soil interaction more effectively, enabling machines trained largely in simulation to adapt robustly to real-world tasks with minimal additional data.

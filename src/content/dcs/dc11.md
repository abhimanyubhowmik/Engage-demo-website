background: |
  I received a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering – Electronics at the University of Science and Culture in Tehran, Iran, and an M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering – Control at Kharazmi University, where I focused on developing RL-based control techniques for nonlinear dynamics and their application on mobile robots.

  My research interests include the application of control theory in autonomous agricultural systems.
project: |
  This project aims to develop and validate a virtual boundary (geofencing) system for autonomous agricultural vehicles. The proposed system ensures that the vehicle remains within a predefined operational boundary and safely prevents unintended exits from the designated area.

  The objectives are to maximise the usable field coverage, minimise the distance between the vehicle’s operating path and the field boundaries, and satisfy the safety requirements associated with different Agricultural Performance Levels (AgPLs).

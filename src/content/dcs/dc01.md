  I have a broad research interest, ranging from control, machine learning and sensor fusion to compressed sensing and neuroscience.
project: |
  How should excavators perceive their surroundings to perform their tasks autonomously? My research focuses on developing multimodal perception frameworks that fuse vision, proprioception, vibration, and pressure signals to understand unstructured dynamic environments, including rocks, soils, pile materials, and other working machines.

  Better understanding of the surrounding environment is essential for improving the robustness of subsequent motion prediction and control strategies.

project: |
  This research project focuses on advancing the autonomy and efficiency of heavy-duty mobile machinery, with a primary application on excavator arms. By integrating deep learning with advanced data-driven control strategies — including offline reinforcement learning and/or imitation learning — the project aims to map complex machine dynamics and automate highly repetitive or hazardous industrial tasks.

  The work is being conducted within the ENGAGE network across Aalto University, Tampere University, and Bosch Rexroth.

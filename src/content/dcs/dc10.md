project: |
  This research focuses on enabling autonomous vehicles to navigate safely in challenging off-road environments where terrain–vehicle interactions are difficult to predict. The project investigates how uncertainties in terrain conditions, vehicle dynamics, and terrain–vehicle contact can be propagated into stochastic dynamics and incorporated into motion planning and control.

  The goal is to enable heavy mobile working machines to navigate safely and efficiently over rough and uneven terrain.

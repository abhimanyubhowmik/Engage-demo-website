  I completed my undergraduate studies in Artificial Intelligence at Amity University Kolkata, India. I then pursued an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master’s degree in Marine and Maritime Intelligent Robotics at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology and Université de Toulon, where my master’s thesis worked on uncertainty-aware mapping and exploration for vision-based underwater robots.

  After graduating, I joined the Czech Institute of Informatics, Robotics and Cybernetics at CTU in Prague as a Junior Researcher, focusing on situational awareness for robotic manipulation.
project: |
  Since the emergence of Vision Language Models, there has been a surge of methods for off-road navigation, specifically self-supervised traversability estimation. My research focuses on understanding these learned traversability models for mobile working machines in dynamic and unstructured forest environments, which can enable autonomous harvesting.

  I am also interested in studying how traversability knowledge can be transferred between different machines and load conditions. Another focus is understanding how environmental factors affect a vehicle’s ability to move safely.
